# Alternative-route audit

This audit checks every route currently represented in the repository. A
finite-dimensional positive matrix or a numerical correlation is not promoted
to an infinite-dimensional theorem.

| Route | Result | Remaining obstruction |
|---|---|---|
| Local positive scalar weight | Scoped formal no-go | A different domain or nonlocal form would be a different problem |
| Global positive `q(D)` multiplier | Analytic no-go under stated hypotheses; `Q=0` | Must abandon global translation invariance or the global form domain |
| Prime-shift multiplier | Positive regularized finite candidates only | Infinite limit, nullspace compatibility, and exact factorization are open |
| Mellin/Gram resolvent space | Null-free positive finite Gram diagnostic | No closed operator, pair symmetry, or complete spectrum theorem |
| de Branges-style kernel | Finite-kernel positivity only | No source-derived Hermite–Biehler function and no exact pair identity |
| Minimal/adjoint domains | Formal weak-domain descriptions | Closed range, endpoint domains, and self-adjoint realization are open |
| Direct energy identity | Formal identity and exact canonical reduction | Global coefficient bounds, endpoint control, and Volterra coercivity are open |
| Half-line trace shortcut | Refuted as a generic complex-parameter inequality | Would require a new consequence of `Xi(alpha)=0`; sine-transform freedom remains |

## Conclusion

The routes do not provide an independent completed proof hidden elsewhere in
the repository. The local and global-multiplier branches are genuine scoped
no-go results. The nonlocal and model-space branches are diagnostics. The
energy branch is the only route with a direct conditional contradiction, but
its missing coercivity theorem remains the central open result.
